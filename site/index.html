A terminal for iPhone, for keeping watch on the coding agents that run on your own computers.

Get it on the App Store Setup guide

iPhone only, iOS 27 or later.

Who it is for

You run agents like Claude Code, Codex or omp on a Mac or Linux computer, and your day often keeps you away from a laptop. Term lets you check on them from your phone: see which one is working, read what it finished, answer its question and let it continue. It is made for supervising work, not for writing code on a phone.

What it does

  • Sessions that survive iOS Term speaks mosh and SSH. A mosh session moves between Wi-Fi and cellular and comes back after iOS suspends or closes the app.
  • tmux, one line per session Every tmux session on your computers in one list. Tap to open, swipe between them. The same sessions stay open on your computer.
  • Agent status Each session shows whether its agent is working, done, failed or asking you something, with the answer or question under its name.
  • Files Browse, search, view and edit files on the computer over SFTP.
  • Dictation Speak a reply instead of typing it, with your own OpenRouter API key.
  • Your Mac's screen Stream the Mac's displays and control them, over your own SSH connection.

What you need

A Mac or Linux computer that stays on and that your phone can reach over SSH: on the same network at home, or from anywhere through Tailscale. There is no account to make. Term connects only to your computers; the developer runs no server that sees your sessions. The setup guide walks through each step.
