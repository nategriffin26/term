Support

Most connection problems are covered in the setup guide's troubleshooting table, which lists the messages Term shows and what to do about each.

Get help

  • Email SUPPORT_EMAIL.
  • Or open an issue on GitHub at REPO_URL/issues. Issues are public, so leave out host addresses, user names, keys and anything private from your terminal.

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (iPhone Settings › General › About).
  • Term's version: Term's Settings › About › Version.
  • Your computer's operating system and its version (macOS, Ubuntu, Fedora…).
  • How the phone reaches it: Tailscale or the same network, and whether the host uses Mosh or SSH.
  • The exact message Term showed, copied or in a screenshot.
  • What you tried, and what happened each time.
  • The output of term-host check on your computer, if you installed the host kit.
