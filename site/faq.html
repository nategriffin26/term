FAQ

Does my terminal leave my machine?

No. Your sessions, keys and files travel only between your iPhone and your own computers, over SSH and mosh. The developer runs no server that sees them. Two exceptions, both described in the privacy policy:

  • If you turn on dictation with your own OpenRouter API key, the audio and the last lines visible in that terminal go to OpenRouter as context for the transcription.
  • If you allow notifications, the name of the session that needs you passes through the developer's relay on its way to Apple's push service.

Do I need a server?

You need a computer of your own that stays on: a Mac or a Linux machine at home or work, or a server you rent. There is no Term service to sign up for. See the setup guide.

Why mosh?

iOS suspends apps in the background and closes them when it needs memory, and phones switch between Wi-Fi and cellular all the time. A plain SSH connection drops when either happens. mosh doesn't: Term saves the session when it goes to the background and picks it up again when you come back, even after iOS closed the app or your network changed. Typing also feels immediate on a slow connection, because Term shows your keystrokes before the computer answers.

Does it work with Claude Code and Codex?

Yes. Any agent runs in Term's terminal. For the tmux tab to show an agent's status (working, done, or asking you something), run the agent inside tmux and install the hooks with term-host install, as in setup step 6. omp is supported through Term's omp extension.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

No. Term is for iPhone only.

Why does Screen need a Mac app?

Term Screen Host captures the Mac's displays and encodes them as hardware video, so the picture stays sharp and fast over a phone connection. It also moves the pointer, types, and shares the clipboard both ways. It streams through your existing SSH connection, so nothing new is open on the network. When the Mac is locked, macOS stops screen capture, so Term switches to the Mac's own Screen Sharing until you unlock it.
